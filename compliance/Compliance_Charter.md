# Compliance Department Charter

**Department:** Compliance  
**Department Owner:** Founder  
**Authority Level:** Advisory

---

## Purpose

The Compliance Department provides compliance framework documentation, audit trail management, documentation standards, and policy enforcement guidance to support Sentinel Labs' operational integrity and regulatory readiness.

---

## Scope of Work (Allowed)

- Compliance framework documentation
- Audit trail and documentation standards
- SOP creation and maintenance
- Payment discipline tracking
- Regulatory calendar management
- Policy enforcement frameworks
- Documentation checklists

---

## Out of Scope (Prohibited)

- Making compliance decisions
- Formal audits or certifications
- Regulatory representation
- Overriding Founder decisions

---

## Authority & Limitations

**Compliance has authority to:**
- Create compliance frameworks
- Establish standards and procedures
- Track compliance metrics
- Recommend policy enforcement

**Compliance does NOT have authority to:**
- Make compliance decisions
- Perform formal audits
- Represent to regulators
- Override Founder decisions

**Final authority on all compliance decisions:** Founder
