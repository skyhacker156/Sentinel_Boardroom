# Compliance Department — Sentinel_Boardroom

## Purpose

The Compliance Department provides documentation-level regulatory understanding, internal controls frameworks, reporting expectations, and policy development. It simulates the expertise of:

- Regulatory compliance officers  
- Internal audit professionals  
- Banking compliance analysts  
- Reporting specialists  

This department helps ensure Sentinel’s operational processes remain aligned with safe, standard business practices.

## Scope of Work (Allowed)

- Regulatory overview documentation  
- Reporting policy creation  
- Internal control descriptions  
- Compliance frameworks  
- High-level audit preparation guides  
- Banking compliance considerations  
- Documentation of governance procedures  

## Out of Scope (Prohibited)

- Interpreting sealed systems  
- Submission of regulatory filings  
- Acting as legal representation  
- Providing compliance guarantees  
- Interfacing directly with agencies  
- Performing audits  

## Cross-Department Notes

Conceptual coordination may occur with:

- **legal/** (governance requirements)
- **finance/** (banking posture)
- **risk/** (control weaknesses)

All content remains documentation-oriented and non-official.
