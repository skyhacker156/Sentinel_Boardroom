# Banking_Relationship_Readiness_Matrix

**Purpose:** Internal assessment framework for evaluating organizational readiness before engaging banking partners.

**Scope:** Strategic planning and self-assessment only. Not financial or legal advice.

**Date:** November 23, 2025

**Audience:** Founder (internal use only)

---

## Matrix Overview

This framework assesses readiness across five domains. For each domain, answer the readiness questions. Score each question 0–5 (0 = not ready, 5 = fully ready). Domain score = average of question scores.

---

**Purpose:** Assess financial stability and track record.

| Question | Score (0–5) | Notes |

| Are accounts payable aging <45 days? | — | Payment discipline indicator |

## Interpretation Guide

**4.5–5.0:** Ready to engage banking partners now. Approach with confidence.

**3.5–4.4:** Largely ready. Address 1–2 weak domains (30–60 days remediation).

**2.5–3.4:** Foundational gaps present. Recommend 60–90 day remediation plan before approach.

**1.5–2.4:** Significant work required. Establish remediation roadmap; revisit in 90 days.

**0–1.4:** Not ready for banking relationship. Focus on Domain 2 (Documentation) and Domain 1 (Financial History) first.

---

## Post-Scoring Action Plan

**For each domain scoring <3.5:**

1. **Identify the 2–3 lowest questions** in that domain.
2. **Assign ownership** (Founder, accountant, attorney, operations lead).
3. **Set a completion date** (30, 60, or 90 days).
4. **Document the remediation** (what was done, by whom, when).
5. **Re-score** after completion.

---

## Notes for Founder

- This matrix is for **internal alignment only**. Do not share raw scores with banks.
- Banks will ask similar questions; prepare answers in advance.
- Score conservatively. A 3.5 on your assessment = a 3.0 to a bank.
- Update this matrix **quarterly** as circumstances change.
- Use this as a diagnostic, not a prediction. Banking relationships depend on fit, timing, and relationship chemistry as much as metrics.
