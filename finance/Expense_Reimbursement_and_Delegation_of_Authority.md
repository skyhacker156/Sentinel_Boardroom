# Expense_Reimbursement_and_Delegation_of_Authority

**Purpose:** Integrated framework for managing expenses, reimbursements, and delegated spending authority.

**Scope:** Internal operational governance. Founder veto point on all policies.

**Date:** November 23, 2025

**Audience:** Founder (internal use only)

---

## Overview

This document establishes:

1. **Expense Reimbursement Policy** — what qualifies, approval process, documentation, timeline
2. **Delegation of Authority Matrix** — who can approve what spending, at what thresholds
3. **Integration Points** — how reimbursement and delegation work together

---

## Part 1: Expense Reimbursement Policy

### 1.1 Eligible Expense Categories

**The following expenses are eligible for reimbursement:**

| Category | Examples | Policy |
|----------|----------|--------|
| **Travel** | Airfare, hotel, ground transport, meals (per diem) | Pre-approval required for >$500 single trip |
| **Professional Services** | Legal, accounting, consulting (pre-approved vendors only) | Invoice + SOW required; paid directly to vendor when possible |
| **Technology & Tools** | Software licenses, subscriptions, hardware, cloud services | Proof of business use; annual licenses preferred |
| **Office & Operations** | Supplies, equipment, furniture, utilities (if not corporate account) | Receipt required; <$1,000 per item unless pre-approved |
| **Client/Vendor Engagement** | Meals, events, gifts (within limits) | Business purpose documented; recipient identified |
| **Training & Development** | Courses, conferences, certifications | Pre-approval required; relevant to role |
| **Insurance & Compliance** | Professional liability, D&O, regulatory fees | Pre-approval required; annual review |

**The following expenses are NOT eligible:**

- Personal expenses (groceries, personal hygiene, entertainment unrelated to business)
- Alcohol (except business meals, capped at 20% of meal cost)
- Parking violations, traffic fines, legal penalties
- Personal vehicle maintenance or insurance
- Home office rent (unless formal home-office arrangement documented)
- Meals for personal household members
- Tips exceeding 20% on meals or services

---

### 1.2 Approval Authority for Reimbursement

**All reimbursement requests must be approved before payment.**

| Expense Amount | Approver | Process | Timeline |
|----------------|----------|---------|----------|
| <$250 | Department Lead or Founder | Email + receipt | 5 business days |
| $250–$1,000 | Department Lead + Finance Review | Email + receipt + business purpose memo | 7 business days |
| $1,000–$5,000 | Founder | Email + receipt + 1-page justification | 10 business days |
| >$5,000 | Founder + external review (if new vendor) | Full documentation + vendor verification | 15 business days |

---

### 1.3 Documentation Requirements

**For all reimbursement requests, provide:**

1. **Original receipt** (digital or scanned) showing:
   - Vendor name and date
   - Item description
   - Amount paid
   - Payment method

2. **Business purpose statement** (1–2 sentences):
   - What was purchased
   - Why it was business-necessary
   - Who benefited (if applicable)

3. **Approval memo** (for >$250):
   - Submitted by (employee/contractor name)
   - Department or project
   - Approval chain (who approved, when)

4. **Proof of payment** (if paid from personal account):
   - Bank or credit card statement excerpt
   - Invoice number
   - Vendor confirmation email

---

### 1.4 Reimbursement Timeline

| Step | Timeline | Responsibility |
|------|----------|-----------------|
| Expense incurred | Day 0 | Team member |
| Reimbursement request submitted | Within 30 days of expense | Team member |
| Approval review | 3–7 business days | Approver |
| Payment processed | 5 business days after approval | Finance/Accounting |
| Funds received | 3–5 business days after processing | Team member |

**Note:** Requests submitted >60 days after expense date require Founder pre-approval before processing.

---

### 1.5 Special Reimbursement Cases

#### **Travel Pre-Approval**

For any trip >$500:
- Submit itinerary + estimated costs before booking
- Approval must come from Founder
- Book through corporate vendor when available (better rates, audit trail)
- Daily meal allowance (per diem): $50 (breakfast), $75 (lunch), $100 (dinner) — or actual receipts, whichever is lower

#### **Home Office / Remote Work Expenses**

- One-time equipment (desk, chair, monitor): pre-approval required; must be business-owned asset
- Internet, utilities: not reimbursable (assumed part of work-from-home arrangement)
- Occasional co-working space: reimbursable with receipt; max $300/month

#### **Professional Development**

- Courses, certifications, conferences: pre-approval required
- Tuition reimbursement: must be approved in advance; document relevance to role
- Books and materials: receipts required; max $50/book unless pre-approved

#### **Client Entertainment & Meals**

- Business meal with prospect/client: reimbursable with receipt
- Attendees must be documented (names, companies, purpose of meeting)
- Per-person spend limit: $75 (unless pre-approved client entertainment budget)
- Alcohol: included in meal allowance, not a separate line item

## Part 2: Delegation of Authority Matrix

### 2.1 Spending Authority by Role

**This matrix defines who can approve and commit funds without escalation.**

| Role | Approval Limit | Scope | Restrictions |
|------|----------------|-------|--------------|
| **Founder** | Unlimited | All categories | None |
| **Finance/Accounting Lead** | $5,000 | Operational expenses, vendor payments, tools | Requires Founder approval for new vendor >$1,000/year |
| **Department Lead** | $1,000 | Team expenses, supplies, software (existing vendors) | Cannot approve own reimbursement; escalate to Founder |
| **Team Member / Contractor** | $250 | Team supplies, small tools, expenses with receipt | Cannot approve own reimbursement; escalate to Department Lead |
| **Temporary / Intern** | $0 | No independent approval authority | All expenses require Department Lead approval |

---

### 2.2 Category-Specific Authority

**Certain categories require additional approval, regardless of amount:**

| Category | <$1,000 | $1,000–$5,000 | >$5,000 |
|----------|---------|---------------|---------|
| **Travel** | Department Lead + Founder | Founder | Founder + CFO review |
| **New Vendor (First-time)** | Founder | Founder + Finance review | Founder + external verification |
| **Professional Services** | Founder | Founder + SOW review | Founder + Board/external review |
| **Recurring Subscriptions** | Department Lead | Founder | Founder + cost-benefit analysis |
| **Equipment/Capital** | Department Lead | Founder | Founder + asset depreciation plan |
| **Charitable / Discretionary** | $0 (Founder only) | Founder | Founder |

---

### 2.3 Escalation Protocol

**If an expense falls outside normal authority:**

1. **Requester** → Submits to their direct approver with clear business case
2. **Direct Approver** → Reviews; if within authority, approves; if not, escalates with recommendation
3. **Next Level** → Escalates to Founder with full documentation and recommendation
4. **Founder** → Makes final decision; documents rationale
5. **Finance** → Logs decision for future reference

**Timeline:** Each escalation step should take <2 business days. Total escalation: <1 week.

---

### 2.4 Purchasing Authority (Procurement)

**Distinct from reimbursement — for direct vendor commitments:**

| Purchase Type | <$1,000 | $1,000–$5,000 | >$5,000 |
|----------------|---------|---------------|---------|
| **Existing vendor** | Department Lead | Finance Lead | Founder |
| **New vendor (first-time)** | Founder | Founder + RFQ process | Founder + competitive bid |
| **Recurring contract** | Finance Lead (renewal) | Founder (renewal) | Founder + Board review |
| **Emergency purchase** | Department Lead (notify Finance same day) | Founder | Founder + post-purchase review |

## Part 3: Integration — Reimbursement & Authority Together

### 3.1 Common Workflow Examples

#### **Example 1: Team Member Books Travel**

1. Team member requests pre-approval from Department Lead (travel >$500)
2. Department Lead escalates to Founder with itinerary + budget
3. Founder approves; team member books
4. Team member submits receipts + per diem claims within 30 days
5. Finance processes reimbursement within 7 days of approval

#### **Example 2: New Software Tool**

1. Department Lead identifies tool need; requests quotes (3 vendors)
2. Submits to Founder: pricing, team size, annual cost
3. Founder approves (if <$5,000/year) or escalates (if >$5,000/year)
4. Finance processes subscription directly with vendor (preferred)
5. If reimbursement needed: submit invoice + approval memo within 30 days

#### **Example 3: Contractor Expense Reimbursement**

1. Contractor incurs expense (supplies, travel, software)
2. Submits receipt + business purpose to Founder within 30 days
3. Founder approves (if <$250) or Finance reviews (if >$250)
4. Finance reimburses within 7 days of approval
5. Contractor documents in next invoice reconciliation

#### **Example 4: Department Lead Needs Equipment**

1. Department Lead requests approval from Founder (with quote)
2. Founder approves + assigns asset ownership tag
3. Finance purchases directly from vendor
4. Asset logged in fixed-asset register
5. No reimbursement needed (direct vendor payment)

---

### 3.2 Compliance & Audit Trail

**All approvals and expenses must maintain an audit trail:**

- **Approval chain** — Who approved, when, amount approved
- **Business justification** — What was the business reason?
- **Supporting docs** — Receipts, quotes, SOWs, contracts
- **Payment proof** — Invoice paid, receipt, reconciliation to ledger
- **Category coding** — Expense categorized for financial reporting

**Quarterly review:** Founder reviews all expenses >$1,000 for reasonableness and alignment with policy.

## Part 4: Special Policies

### 4.1 Personal vs. Corporate Cards

**If using corporate card:**
- Monthly statement review required
- Personal expenses must be reimbursed to company within 30 days
- Recurring subscriptions should be billed directly to corporate card (not personal + reimbursement)

**If using personal card (temporary):**
- Reimbursement request must be submitted within 30 days
- Receipt + business purpose required
- >2 reimbursements/month suggests need for corporate card

### 4.2 Foreign Currency & International Expenses

- Reimbursement at exchange rate on date of expense (not date of reimbursement)
- Documentation: receipt + currency conversion proof
- Higher approval limits may apply due to currency fluctuation risk

### 4.3 Founder Expenses

- Founder approves own expenses up to $25,000 (documented for tax/compliance)
- >$25,000 requires Board or external advisor review
- All personal/household expenses clearly separated from business expenses

### 4.4 Vendor Performance & Recurring Commitments

- Before renewing any contract >$5,000/year, conduct vendor performance review
- Document: cost, service quality, alternatives considered
- Founder approval required for multi-year commitments or auto-renew clauses

## Part 5: Exception & Override Handling

### 5.1 Emergency Expenses

**If immediate action is needed (cannot wait for approval):**

1. Team member makes expense, documents business urgency
2. Notifies approver same day with email explaining
3. Submits full documentation + justification within 48 hours
4. Approver reviews + either approves retroactively or requests reimbursement
5. Founder notified for >$1,000 emergency expenses

### 5.2 Policy Waivers

**If an expense doesn't fit policy but has strong business justification:**

1. Requester submits to Founder with full context
2. Founder documents waiver decision (approval memo)
3. Finance processes reimbursement or direct payment
4. Waiver logged in exception register (reviewed quarterly)

### 5.3 Dispute Resolution

**If approver and requester disagree on reimbursement:**

1. Requester escalates to next level with written rationale
2. Approver submits written counter-rationale
3. Founder makes final decision
4. Decision documented for future reference

---

## Part 6: Quarterly & Annual Review

### 6.1 Quarterly Financial Review

- Total expenses by category (trend analysis)
- Largest expenses and approvers
- Policy violations or gray areas
- Vendor concentration (if >20% from single vendor, flag for diversification)

### 6.2 Annual Policy Review

- Update approval limits based on company growth
- Eliminate unused categories
- Refine per-diem or meal allowances
- Add new categories as needed

### 6.3 Founder Accountability

- All expenses >$5,000 reviewed by Founder quarterly
- All new vendors >$10,000/year approved by Founder
- Any policy waiver >$2,500 logged and reported to Founder

---

## Notes for Founder

- This policy is **flexible by design**. You can override it at any time with documented rationale.
- The goal is **clarity and audit trail**, not bureaucracy.
- As the company grows, you may delegate approval authority to a CFO or Finance Lead — this matrix provides the framework.
- All policies should be reviewed **annually** and adjusted for business reality.
- **Transparency** is non-negotiable — if a team member or vendor relationship requires secrecy around spending, that's a signal to escalate to Founder.
