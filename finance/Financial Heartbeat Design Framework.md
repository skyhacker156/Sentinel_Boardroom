### Executive Summary
The Financial Heartbeat is the deliberate, recurring pattern of payments and obligations that establishes Sentinel Labs’ commercial payment history with credit bureaus (Dun & Bradstreet, Experian Commercial, Equifax Business, CreditorWatch, etc.).  
When engineered correctly from Day 1, a properly structured heartbeat becomes the single highest-ROI activity for corporate credit formation — routinely accelerating Paydex 80 achievement from 12–18 months (organic) to 90–180 days.

### Core Principles of Heartbeat Design (2025–2026 Environment)
1. Predictability > Volume  
2. Bureau-Reporting > Non-Reporting Vendors  
3. Automation > Manual Payments  
4. Diversification of Creditor Types (5–12 distinct reporting lines ideal)  
5. Graduated Scaling (start small, prove reliability, then increase limits)

### Payment Routing & Automation Blueprint
- Primary Operating Account → Mercury or Brex (free ACH/wires)  
- Scheduled ACH pulls 10 days before due date (never push payments — pulls report better)  
- Use Bill.com or Ramp for centralized routing and audit trail (both report to bureaus when used correctly)  
- Calendarize “early pay” discounts where available (further boosts scores)
