Cash_Flow_Policy_Template.md

Finance • Documentation-Only Template • Version 1.0

Purpose

This template defines the structure for documenting internal cash-flow policies.
It is intended for planning, review, and boardroom visibility only.
No execution, automation, or financial instructions are included.

1. Policy Overview

Objective:
Document the guiding principles for monitoring, forecasting, and maintaining stable corporate cash flow.

Scope:

Applies to general operations

Covers high-level planning only

Requires Founder approval before activation

2. Cash Flow Components
2.1 Inflows (Conceptual Categories)

Revenue streams (non-specific)

Receivables cadence

Expected settlement timelines

Predictable financing or capital injections

2.2 Outflows (Conceptual Categories)

Operating expenses

Fixed commitments

Strategic expenditures

Credit-building payments

Compliance-required disbursements

3. Monitoring Policies
3.1 Reporting Rhythm

Monthly high-level review

Quarterly boardroom summary

Documentation-first approach

3.2 Indicators to Observe

Balance stability

Variance vs. expected cadence

Burn-to-inflow alignment

Ability to meet reporting vendor obligations

Early detection of atypical utilization patterns

4. Forecasting Guidelines
4.1 Planning Horizon

Short-term: 30–60 days

Mid-term: 90–180 days

Long-term (conceptual): 6–12 months

4.2 Assumptions (Documented Only)

No projections of revenue

No guarantees

No numerical forecasting

Qualitative patterns only

5. Risk Controls
5.1 Safeguards

Maintain conceptual cushion (non-numerical)

Prioritize credit-building payments

Document all deviations from expected cadence

5.2 Escalation Criteria

Escalate to Founder review if:

Outflow categories expand faster than inflow categories

Several reporting vendors approach simultaneous due dates

Credit-aligned spend becomes inconsistent

6. Recordkeeping Requirements

Maintain clear annotation for every cash-flow category

Store all policy revisions under version control

Log updates in Boardroom Index when created

7. Policy Review Cycle

Suggested review: once per quarter

Updates require Founder approval

Remove outdated assumptions on each revision

9. Notes
