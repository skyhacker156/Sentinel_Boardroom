# Finance Department — Sentinel_Boardroom

## Purpose

The Finance Department provides documentation-level financial strategy, credit architecture planning, banking readiness, and operational financial frameworks to support Sentinel Labs. This department simulates the expertise of:

- Financial strategists  
- Banking relationship officers  
- Corporate finance analysts  
- Credit architects  
- Treasury operations personnel  

This department supports the Founder in establishing Sentinel’s initial financial footprint, ongoing payment patterns, and long-term credit formation.

## Scope of Work (Allowed)

- Credit architecture frameworks  
- Vendor onboarding strategies  
- Banking readiness documentation  
- Financial heartbeat planning  
- Capital structure overviews  
- Expense categorization (documentation-level)  
- Non-advisory financial analysis  
- Treasury workflow documentation  
- Payment routing design  

## Out of Scope (Prohibited)

- Personalized investment advice  
- Executing banking operations  
- Accessing founder’s personal financial data  
- Treasury execution or fund movement  
- Anything involving proprietary S³ logic  

## Cross-Department Notes

The Finance Department may reference:

- **legal/** (for liability structure)
- **tax/** (for deductible category logic)
- **risk/** (for financial risk exposure)
- **compliance/** (for banking policy alignment)

All work must remain in documentation form only.
