## Critical Risks (Finance-Specific)

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Prevention | Mitigation | Owner | Status |
|------|------------|--------|-----------|-----------|-------|--------|
| **Capital deployment delay** (impacts S³ execution timeline) | Medium | High | Monthly capital deployment review, clear timelines documented | Execute immediately when Founder approves, maintain backup plan | Finance + Founder | Active |
| **Payment discipline lapse** (missed vendor payment, Paydex collapses) | Low | Critical | Automated payment calendar, compliance SOP, weekly checks | Immediate payment if missed, Paydex recovery plan (if needed) | Finance + Compliance | Active |
| **Vendor relationship failure** (vendor closes account, credit disrupted) | Low | Medium | Vendor diversification, relationship monitoring, backup vendors | Activate backup vendors immediately, rebuild tradelines | Finance | Active |
| **Paydex score degradation** (lenders see negative payment pattern) | Low | High | Perfect on-time payment discipline, no missed payments ever | Immediate remediation, communication with lenders | Finance | Active |
| **Lender fraud detection** (Brex/Ramp flag account as suspicious) | Very Low | High | Legitimate operational spending, clear business purpose documentation | Appeal to lender, explain legitimate use, recover account | Finance + Founder | Active |
| **Regulatory change** (new banking rules affect strategy) | Medium | Medium | Quarterly regulatory review, Legal coordination | Adapt strategy, revise timelines, document changes | Finance + Legal | Active |
| **Mercury account issues** (Mercury closes account, Plaid disconnects) | Very Low | Medium | Maintain adequate balance, legitimate activity, compliance | Migrate to traditional bank immediately, rebuild Plaid connection | Finance | Active |
| **Tax compliance failure** (IRS/state tax issue impacts deductibility) | Low | Medium | Tax Department coordination, documentation, quarterly review | Work with CPA, remediate immediately, document resolution | Finance + Tax | Active |
| **Capital access denial** (next funding round rejected) | Low | Medium | Document strong credit history, maintain Paydex 75+, demonstrate usage | Build alternative capital sources, extend timeline if needed | Finance | Active |
| **Internal documentation gap** (missing audit trail, compliance issue) | Low | Medium | Compliance SOP enforcement, documentation standards | Reconstruct documentation, implement process improvements | Finance + Compliance | Active |

## Risk Escalation Protocol

If any risk materializes:

1. **Detect** — Finance notices the issue (e.g., payment failed)
2. **Alert** — Immediately notify Founder
3. **Assess** — Determine severity and immediate impact
4. **Respond** — Execute mitigation from Risk Register
5. **Document** — Log what happened and how it was resolved
6. **Review** — Post-incident review to prevent recurrence
7. **Update** — Revise risk register if new insights emerge
