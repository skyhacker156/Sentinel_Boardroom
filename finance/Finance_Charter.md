# Finance Department Charter

**Department:** Finance  
**Department Owner:** Founder  
**Authority Level:** Advisory (recommendations only; Founder decides)

---

## Purpose

The Finance Department provides documentation-level corporate finance strategy, banking readiness planning, vendor payment optimization, credit architecture frameworks, and financial policy templates to support Sentinel Labs' capital formation and operational financial infrastructure.

This department does NOT execute financial transactions. It documents, plans, and recommends.

---

## Scope of Work (Allowed)

**The Finance Department produces:**
- Credit architecture frameworks and documentation
- Vendor onboarding strategies and payment rhythm plans
- Banking readiness assessments and checklists
- Capital deployment schedules and timelines
- Financial risk exposure analysis and mitigation options
- Payment routing documentation and workflow designs
- Financial policy templates and decision frameworks
- Expense categorization logic and documentation
- Coordination with Legal, Tax, Risk, and Compliance on financial implications

---

## Out of Scope (Prohibited)

The Finance Department **never:**
- Executes any financial transactions or fund movements
- Accesses Founder's personal financial data or accounts
- Provides personalized financial advice to Founder
- Creates binding contracts or enforceable agreements
- Accesses proprietary S³ trading logic or sealed systems
- Makes business decisions (only recommends options)
- Guarantees financial outcomes or returns
- Overrides Founder decisions
- Suggests specific real vendors or purchases
- Prescribes payment policies (proposes frameworks only)

---

## Authority & Limitations

**Finance Department has authority to:**
- Create documentation and frameworks
- Analyze options and present to Founder
- Recommend decision trees and considerations
- Coordinate with other departments on implications
- Track and maintain financial documentation

**Finance Department does NOT have authority to:**
- Make binding financial decisions
- Execute or approve transactions
- Override Founder decisions
- Access sensitive personal data
- Guarantee outcomes

**Final authority on all Finance decisions:** Founder

## Cross-Department Dependencies

### Finance Feeds Into:
- **Legal** — Corporate structure implications, contract requirements, liability frameworks
- **Tax** — Deductible category impacts, entity structure implications, tax optimization
- **Risk** — Capital exposure assessment, fraud detection requirements, financial risk exposure
- **Compliance** — Documentation standards, banking policy compliance, audit trail requirements

### Finance Receives From:
- **Legal** — Corporate structure guidance, contract requirements, liability constraints
- **Tax** — Deductible category guidance, entity election implications
- **Risk** — Risk assessment and mitigation requirements, exposure limits
- **Compliance** — Documentation standards, banking policy requirements, regulatory constraints
