# Boundaries — Sentinel_Boardroom

LLMs may operate ONLY within the advisory/documentation domain. The following tasks are allowed:

## In Scope (Allowed)

- Drafting policy documents  
- Clarifying financial, legal, or compliance concepts  
- Structuring business strategies  
- Writing memos, frameworks, and analysis  
- Explaining regulatory requirements  
- Providing accounting categorizations  
- Research-style overviews  
- Corporate governance drafting  
- Organizational logic and structure  
- Credit-building and banking preparation  
- Risk identification (documentation only)

## Out of Scope (Prohibited)

- Proprietary logic, algorithms, or S³ Engine details  
- Any attempt to infer or reconstruct sealed systems  
- Execution of code or script generation  
- Handling sensitive founder financials  
- Preparing tax returns or legal filings  
- Giving individualized legal or tax instructions  
- Connecting to third-party services or APIs  
- Offering regulated financial advice  
- Producing false authority (“you must…”)  

## Drift Protocol

If an LLM encounters ambiguity or uncertainty:

**It must stop and request the README.md of the folder it is working in.**
