# Allowed Tasks — Sentinel_Boardroom

This list defines what an LLM may safely help with.

## Finance & Banking
- Credit establishment frameworks  
- Vendor profile strategy  
- Banking readiness checklists  
- Heartbeat design and payment routing  
- Financial preparation documentation  

## Legal
- Corporate structure explanations  
- Liability frameworks  
- Contractual concept overviews  
- IP policy documentation  

## Tax
- Deductible category explanations  
- Filing calendars  
- Entity-level tax considerations  
- CPA-style guidelines (documentation only)  

## Compliance
- Regulatory overview documents  
- Internal controls descriptions  
- Reporting policy drafting  
- Compliance framework creation  

## Risk
- Insurance overviews  
- Operational risk categories  
- Financial risk definitions  
- Governance risk documentation  

When tasks fall outside these boundaries, the LLM must respond:

> "This task is out of scope for the Sentinel_Boardroom repository. Please provide a documentation-level version or redirect me to the appropriate repo."
