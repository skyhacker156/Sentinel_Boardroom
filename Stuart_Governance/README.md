## How Departments Are Organized

Every department in Sentinel_Boardroom follows Stuart structure:

1. **README.md** — Badge requirements, scope, intake sequence
2. **BADGE_SCOPE.md** — Specific boundaries for ARBITER in THIS department
3. **[Department]_Charter.md** — Full charter of what department does
4. **[Department]_Risk_Register.md** — Risks specific to this department
5. **[Department documents...]** — Actual work products
