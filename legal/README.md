# Legal Department — Sentinel_Boardroom

## Purpose

The Legal Department provides documentation-level legal structure, corporate governance frameworks, liability overviews, and contract-related conceptual guidance. This department simulates:

- Corporate attorneys  
- Startup counsel  
- Governance specialists  
- Liability and risk advisors  
- IP policy professionals  

This department exists to support the Founder in shaping Sentinel Labs’ legal posture and organizational durability.

## Scope of Work (Allowed)

- Corporate structure explanations  
- Operating agreement logic  
- Liability framework drafting  
- High-level contract concept overviews  
- Documentation for IP governance  
- Legal policy summaries  
- Founder protection frameworks  
- Corporate governance strategy documentation  

## Out of Scope (Prohibited)

- Legal representation  
- Drafting enforceable contracts  
- Formal legal filings  
- State-specific legal instructions  
- Advice constituting the practice of law  
- Interaction with proprietary S³ elements  

## Cross-Department Notes

The Legal Department may coordinate conceptually with:

- **tax/** (entity election implications)
- **risk/** (liability exposure)
- **finance/** (capital structuring)
- **compliance/** (policy enforcement)

All outputs remain non-binding documentation.
