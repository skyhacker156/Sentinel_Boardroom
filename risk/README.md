# Risk Department — Sentinel_Boardroom

## Purpose

The Risk Department provides documentation-level analysis across operational, financial, legal, and strategic risk categories. It simulates:

- Enterprise risk analysts  
- Insurance advisors  
- Operational risk specialists  
- Banking and liquidity risk analysts  

Its role is to support the Founder in identifying potential exposures and preparing stabilization frameworks for Sentinel Labs.

## Scope of Work (Allowed)

- Operational risk analysis  
- Financial risk documentation  
- Liability and governance risk overviews  
- Insurance category descriptions  
- Business continuity concepts  
- High-level exposure assessments  
- Internal safeguard frameworks  

## Out of Scope (Prohibited)

- Formal risk certification  
- Drafting insurance policies  
- Specific actuarial analysis  
- Accessing sealed or proprietary systems  
- Personalized risk guidance  
- Interacting with external agencies  

## Cross-Department Notes

The Risk Department may conceptually collaborate with:

- **legal/** (liability and governance)
- **compliance/** (control effectiveness)
- **finance/** (financial exposure)
- **tax/** (operational compliance)

All content produced is advisory documentation only.
