# Risk Department Charter

**Department:** Risk  
**Department Owner:** Founder  
**Authority Level:** Advisory

---

## Purpose

The Risk Department provides risk identification, exposure mapping, mitigation frameworks, and operational risk assessment to support Sentinel Labs' operational resilience and strategic risk management.

---

## Scope of Work (Allowed)

- Risk identification and mapping
- Failure mode analysis
- Exposure assessment frameworks
- Mitigation strategy documentation
- Risk monitoring and tracking
- Scenario analysis
- Contingency planning

---

## Out of Scope (Prohibited)

- Making risk decisions
- Guaranteeing risk outcomes
- Formal risk certification
- Binding risk commitments

---

## Authority & Limitations

**Risk has authority to:**
- Identify and map risks
- Analyze exposure
- Recommend mitigations
- Monitor risk indicators

**Risk does NOT have authority to:**
- Make risk decisions
- Override Founder decisions
- Guarantee outcomes

**Final authority on all risk decisions:** Founder

---

## Documents Risk Owns

All strategic risk documentation
