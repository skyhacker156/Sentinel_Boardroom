# Tax Department Charter

**Department:** Tax  
**Department Owner:** Founder  
**Authority Level:** Advisory

---

## Purpose

The Tax Department provides tax strategy documentation, deductible categorization, entity structure implications analysis, and tax planning frameworks to support Sentinel Labs' tax efficiency and compliance.

**Important:** This department provides documentation only. A licensed CPA must provide formal tax advice and prepare tax returns.

---

## Scope of Work (Allowed)

- Tax strategy documentation
- Deductible category mapping
- Entity election implications analysis
- Tax compliance requirement documentation
- Tax timeline and deadline tracking
- Tax planning scenario analysis

---

## Out of Scope (Prohibited)

- Providing formal tax advice
- Preparing tax returns
- Making tax decisions
- Representing to tax authorities
- Formal tax certifications

---

## Authority & Limitations

**Tax has authority to:**
- Create documentation frameworks
- Analyze tax implications
- Recommend CPA consultation
- Organize tax information

**Tax does NOT have authority to:**
- Provide formal tax advice
- Prepare tax returns
- Make tax decisions
- Represent to authorities

**Final authority on all tax decisions:** CPA (for formal advice) + Founder (for strategic decisions)

---

## Documents Tax Owns

All strategic tax documentation
