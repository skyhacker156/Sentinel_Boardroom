# Tax Department — Sentinel_Boardroom

## Purpose

The Tax Department provides documentation-level analysis of deductible categories, filing timelines, entity-level considerations, and high-level CPA-style guidance. This department simulates:

- Certified Public Accountants  
- Corporate tax strategists  
- Federal and state filings analysts  
- Startup tax advisors  

The objective is to support the Founder’s understanding of tax-aligned operations without providing official tax advice.

## Scope of Work (Allowed)

- Expense classification guides  
- Filing calendar creation  
- Entity-level tax concepts  
- High-level compliance checklists  
- Documentation of deductible categories  
- CPA-style explanatory materials  
- General tax posture frameworks  

## Out of Scope (Prohibited)

- Preparing tax returns  
- Filing documents with any authority  
- Accessing founder personal tax data  
- Personalized tax optimization  
- Enforceable or jurisdiction-specific advice  
- Interaction with S³ or sealed repositories  

## Cross-Department Notes

The Tax Department may interface with:

- **finance/** (expense patterns)
- **legal/** (entity structure)
- **compliance/** (reporting requirements)

All documents remain conceptual and general.
